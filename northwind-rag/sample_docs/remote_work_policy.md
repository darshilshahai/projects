# Northwind Labs — Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their three-month probation period are eligible to work remotely. Contractors and interns are not covered by this policy and should follow the arrangements agreed with their reporting manager.

## Days allowed

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. At least two days per week must be spent in the office, and Wednesday is a fixed company-wide in-office day for all teams. Any exception to the Wednesday rule must be approved in writing by a department head.

## Home office stipend

Every eligible remote employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 25,000 rupees to set up their workspace. This stipend can be used for a desk, a chair, a monitor, or other approved equipment. Receipts must be submitted within thirty days of purchase to be reimbursed.

## Internet allowance

In addition to the one-time stipend, remote employees receive a monthly internet allowance of 1,500 rupees. This allowance is paid automatically with the monthly salary and does not require receipts.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop to every employee. Remote employees who need a second monitor or an external keyboard may request one through the IT portal. All company equipment must be returned when an employee leaves the company.
