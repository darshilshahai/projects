# Northwind Labs — Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to twenty-one days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Annual leave must be requested at least seven days in advance through the leave portal, and it must be approved by the reporting manager before the leave begins.

## Carry forward

Employees may carry forward a maximum of five unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Any unused days beyond five are forfeited on the thirty-first of December and are not paid out.

## Sick leave

Every employee receives twelve days of paid sick leave per year. For any sick leave longer than two consecutive days, the employee must submit a medical certificate from a registered doctor. Sick leave cannot be carried forward and does not get paid out.

## Parental leave

Birth mothers are entitled to twenty-six weeks of paid maternity leave. Fathers and partners are entitled to two weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within three months of the child's birth. Adoption leave follows the same terms as maternity and paternity leave.

## Bereavement leave

Employees may take up to five days of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family is defined as a spouse, child, parent, or sibling.
