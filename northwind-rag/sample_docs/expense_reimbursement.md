# Northwind Labs — Expense Reimbursement Policy

## What can be claimed

Employees can claim reimbursement for expenses that are directly related to their work. This includes business travel, client meals, approved software subscriptions, and conference tickets. Personal expenses, fines, and alcohol are never reimbursed.

## Travel

For domestic business travel, employees may book economy-class flights and three-star or four-star hotels. Any hotel booking above 8,000 rupees per night requires prior approval from a department head. Airport taxi fares are reimbursed in full with a receipt.

## Meals

During business travel, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 1,200 rupees. Meals above this amount are the employee's own responsibility. Client meals, where the employee hosts a customer, are reimbursed separately and are not counted against the daily allowance.

## Approval process

All expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within fifteen days of the expense. Claims above 10,000 rupees require manager approval before they are paid. Claims below 10,000 rupees are auto-approved but may be audited later.

## Payment timeline

Approved expense claims are paid out with the next monthly salary cycle. If a claim is submitted after the payroll cut-off date, which is the twentieth of each month, it will be paid in the following month's cycle.
